Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Walkie account and the personal data associated with it. It applies to the Walkie mobile app, published by 137 WEB DESIGN Ltd.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Walkie app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and associated data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address linked to your account:

Email: hello.walkie.app@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my account

We verify the request against your account email and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name, email, username, login details).
  • Your posts, lost-pet reports, sightings and comments.
  • Your pets saved in the app.
  • Photos you uploaded.
  • Your push-notification token and session/device records.

What may be kept

  • Anonymised or aggregated data that no longer identifies you (for example leaderboard totals).
  • Limited records we are required to retain for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons, for the period required by law.

For more detail on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
